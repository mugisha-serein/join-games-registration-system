1. Community Rules

Ninja Legion values teamwork, learning, discipline, and community. Every member must follow the region rules and respect fellow members.

2. Respectful Behaviour

Harassment, spam, threats, toxic language, discrimination, and disrespectful behaviour are not allowed. Members must communicate responsibly and protect the safety of the community.

3. Data Use Notice

Your name, phone number, join choice, and comment are used only to review your registration, manage membership, and send relevant Ninja Legion updates. Your personal information will not be sold to third parties.

4. Approval Process

Every registration is submitted with a pending status. Authorized administrators review each request and may approve or reject it according to the community requirements.

5. Ban and Removal Policy

Members who violate the community rules may be suspended, banned, or removed. Attempts to bypass a ban or submit misleading information may also result in permanent restriction.

6. Administrator Moderation

Authorized administrators may approve, reject, ban, remove, or restore registrations when necessary to protect the Ninja Legion community and enforce these rules.
